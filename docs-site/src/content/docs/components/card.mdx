### Use when

<ul><li>{"Use a card to display content and actions on a single topic."}<br />{"Cards should be easy to scan for relevant and actionable information."}</li><li>{"Cards can serve as entry points into deeper levels of detail or navigation, such as a music album or details on an upcoming vacation."}</li><li>{"Cards can be displayed together in a grid, vertical list, or carousel."}</li><li>{"Use cards to contain related elements"}</li><li>{"Contents can include anything from images to headlines, supporting text, buttons, and lists"}</li><li>{"Can also contain other components in slot area."}</li><li>{"Cards have flexible layouts and dimensions based on their contents. Configuration is done at subcomponent level: CardLayout."}</li></ul>

### Don't use when

<ul><li>{"Don’t scroll within a card to reveal information"}</li><li>{"Cards shouldn’t contain content that can be swiped, such as an image carousel or pagination. Also, swipe gestures shouldn’t cause portions of cards to detach upon swiping."}</li><li>{"Don’t let cards bump other elements out of the way. When a card is picked up, it appears in front of all elements, except app bars and navigation."}</li><li>{"On a mobile device, cards can't internally scroll, as it could cause two scroll bars to be displayed."}</li></ul>

### Best practice

<ul><li>{"Adjust the card layout so content remains the main focus on large screens"}</li><li>{"When designing for large screens, use multiple columns to display content"}</li><li>{"Certain devices or user contexts require different components to meet platform expectations"}</li><li>{"Expand a card to reveal information"}</li><li>{"A card should only have one swipe action assigned to it"}</li><li>{"When moving a card, increase its elevation"}</li><li>{"On a mobile device, cards can expand to reveal more content, scrolling within the screen. Content within cards doesn’t scroll."}</li></ul>

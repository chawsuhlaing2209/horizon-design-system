### When to use it

- Use a card to display content and actions on a single topic.<br />Cards should be easy to scan for relevant and actionable information.
- Cards can serve as entry points into deeper levels of detail or navigation, such as a music album or details on an upcoming vacation.
- Cards can be displayed together in a grid, vertical list, or carousel.
- Use cards to contain related elements
- Contents can include anything from images to headlines, supporting text, buttons, and lists
- Can also contain other components in slot area.
- Cards have flexible layouts and dimensions based on their contents. Configuration is done at subcomponent level: CardLayout.

**Where it goes.** In a three-column grid of search results: three vertical cards side by side, one per stay ([InResultsGrid](https://horizon-design-system-delta.vercel.app/?path=/story/components-card--in-results-grid)). In a vertical list: two horizontal cards stacked in a column, each with an outlined "Book" Button in its slot ([InListWithButton](https://horizon-design-system-delta.vercel.app/?path=/story/components-card--in-list-with-button)).

<Aside type="caution" title="When not to use it">

- Don’t scroll within a card to reveal information
- Cards shouldn’t contain content that can be swiped, such as an image carousel or pagination. Also, swipe gestures shouldn’t cause portions of cards to detach upon swiping.
- Don’t let cards bump other elements out of the way. When a card is picked up, it appears in front of all elements, except app bars and navigation.
- On a mobile device, cards can't internally scroll, as it could cause two scroll bars to be displayed.

</Aside>

### Best practice

- Adjust the card layout so content remains the main focus on large screens
- When designing for large screens, use multiple columns to display content
- Certain devices or user contexts require different components to meet platform expectations
- Expand a card to reveal information
- A card should only have one swipe action assigned to it
- When moving a card, increase its elevation
- On a mobile device, cards can expand to reveal more content, scrolling within the screen. Content within cards doesn’t scroll.

### The variant matrix

**Card** (`34:317`) — `state` (`enable` · `hover`). State = **2 variants**.

**cardLayout** (`8:1251`) — `orientation` (`vertical` · `horizontal`), `hasSlot` (`true` · `false`), `slot` (slot). Orientation = **2 variants**.

**cardImage** (`8:1186`) — `state` (`idle` · `hover`), `ratio` (`4:3` · `1:1`), `overlayAction` (`true` · `false`). State × Ratio = **4 variants**.

**cardText** (`8:778`) — `metadata` (`true` · `false`), `review` (`true` · `false`), `price` (`true` · `false`).

Every variant in the Figma set has a story. That is the contract between design and code: a variant with no story is a guaranteed QA failure, and a story with no variant behind it is something nobody designed.
